📖 今天學一個詞📖 Word of the day

VIX（恐慌指數）VIX (the fear index)

它衡量投資人有多害怕。平常大約 15～20，超過 30 代表很恐慌。有句老話說「別人恐懼時我貪婪」：歷史上 VIX 很高的時候，常是長期投資人分批買進的時機。但 VIX 高不代表馬上會反彈，所以才要分批，不要一次買完。

It measures how scared investors are. It usually sits around 15 to 20; above 30 means real fear. As the saying goes, be greedy when others are fearful: a high VIX has often been a good time for long-term investors to buy in stages. But it does not mean an immediate rebound, which is why you buy in stages.
